About

I've always wanted to know how things work, how they're integrated and how they interact. Mechatronics gave me the first half of that: how to wire a sensor, write the firmware and design the part that holds it all together. That curiosity has pulled me through robotics, wearable and assistive tech, aerospace and spacesuit design, and a lot of late nights in the lab making things move.

BCII gave me the second half. It's UTS's transdisciplinary degree, where you work with people from completely different fields to untangle messy, real-world problems. It taught me to sit with ambiguity, talk to people before building anything, and test ideas out in the world. I've used it to launch start-up ideas, design sustainable fashion and run live pop-ups. Now I bring both halves to everything I make.

Design in Mechanical & Mechatronic Systems2026

Textile Sensing Stroke Rehab Sleeve

Stroke can leave the arm weak and spastic, with reduced range of motion, which limits independence. Most rehab devices focus on the hand, so the upper arm is far less explored.

Building on earlier research into textile-integrated flex and stretch sensors, we knitted a comfortable sleeve that reads biceps and triceps activity during elbow movement. EMG and flex sensors detect residual muscle activation and movement intent, and a GUI compares intended against achieved movement.

  • Next: assisted elbow flexion and extension
  • Future: integration with glove-based rehab devices
